Privacy Policy

Transparent guidelines on how DWWH PG collects, protects, processes, and respects resident information across our mobile app and backend services.

Application: DWWH PG (Flutter App) • Organization: Divya Women's Hostel (DWWH PG) • Effective Date: October 2026 • Contact: dwwh.chennai@gmail.com

1 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to DWWH PG (referred to herein as "DWWH PG", "we", "us", or "our"). We operate the DWWH PG mobile application (built using Flutter) and the associated backend management platform designed to streamline accommodation, room allocation, digital rent payments, maintenance requests, and administrative operations for residents and hostel administration.

This Privacy Policy informs residents, prospective occupants, and guests regarding our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of Personal Data when using our mobile app and services. By accessing or using the DWWH PG app, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to the collection and handling of your data in accordance with this policy.

2 Information We Collect

To provide safe, secure, and seamless accommodation services, we collect information that you directly provide to us or that is generated during your stay:

  • Personal Identity & Contact Information: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, permanent and current residential address, workplace/college affiliation, and emergency contact details (primary & secondary names and phone numbers).
  • Government-Issued ID & KYC Documents: Photographic copies or document numbers of identity proof (such as Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) and resident profile photos uploaded during onboarding. This information is legally mandated for tenant identification, resident safety, and statutory local police verification requirements.
  • Accommodation & Tenancy Records: Room number, bed allocation, floor/block, check-in date, check-out/vacation notice date, monthly rental tariff, security deposit amount, and room status.
  • Billing, Fees & Payment Data: Transaction references, rent payment history, security deposit receipts, billing schedules, and digital payment confirmations. Note: All electronic card or UPI payments are processed via certified, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. DWWH PG does not store sensitive banking PINs, CVVs, or payment passwords.
  • Leave Requests & Gate Passes: Planned vacation dates, check-out and expected return dates, emergency travel reasons, and approval logs managed through the resident portal.
  • Maintenance & Service Requests: Support tickets submitted by residents regarding room, electrical, plumbing, or facility repairs, including textual descriptions and optional uploaded photographs showing the issue.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, IP address, system access logs, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push tokens required to deliver real-time notifications (such as fee receipts, dues reminders, gate pass approvals, and hostel announcements).

3 Mobile App Device Permissions

The DWWH PG Flutter mobile app may request the following device permissions only when necessary for specific features:

Permission Purpose Required / Optional
Camera Allows you to take live photographs for your resident profile, KYC identity proof capture, or capturing images of maintenance issues. Optional (Feature-based)
Photo Library / Storage Allows you to select and upload existing digital documents (ID proof, rent payment receipts, complaint images). Optional (Feature-based)
Push Notifications Delivers essential notices including rent invoices, payment receipts, leave approval status, and emergency alerts. Optional (Recommended)

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's operating system settings (iOS / Android).

4 How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate operational, security, and contractual purposes:

  • To register, verify, onboard, and allocate beds/rooms to residents within DWWH PG premises.
  • To generate monthly rental invoices, log security deposits, verify receipts, and maintain accurate accounting ledgers.
  • To ensure resident safety, manage hostel access, verify emergency contacts in health or safety contingencies, and comply with mandatory local police tenant verification protocols.
  • To process resident leave requests, vacation notices, and room vacating settlements.
  • To resolve facility repair complaints, maintenance tickets, and track resolution progress.
  • To dispatch administrative notices, system notifications, SMS/WhatsApp alerts, and push updates.
🛡️

Zero Ad Tracking & No Sale of Personal Data:

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. Your data is used exclusively to administer hostel residency and services.

5 Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party providers who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements to operate our infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase / FCM: Push notification routing and delivery to ensure residents receive real-time alerts.
  • Cloud & Database Hosting: Secure cloud servers with enterprise-grade firewalls, restricted IP whitelisting, and encrypted database volumes.
  • SMS & Communication Gateways: Transactional SMS and WhatsApp dispatchers for sending OTP authentications, invoice summaries, and dues reminders.
  • Statutory Authorities: Where compelled by valid legal process, judicial order, or law enforcement investigations under Indian jurisdiction (e.g., tenant police verification).

6 Data Security & Storage

We take the security of your data seriously and enforce technical and administrative safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All communications between the DWWH PG Flutter mobile app and our backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption.
  • Encrypted Passwords: Resident and administrator authentication credentials are cryptographic one-way hashes (bcrypt/argon2); plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Only authorized DWWH PG administrative staff have access to resident records, and access is monitored through audit logs.

7 Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your personal data for as long as you maintain an active residency or account with DWWH PG. Once your residency ends or you request account closure:

  • Your mobile app login, profile photos, FCM push tokens, and active sessions are permanently deleted.
  • Financial transactions and invoices are retained as legally mandated for tax, accounting, and statutory audit obligations under Indian law (up to 7 years).
  • Police verification records and tenancy contracts are retained only for the required statutory dispute/audit period (typically 180 days post-move-out), after which they are securely purged.

For step-by-step instructions on submitting an account or data deletion request, please view our dedicated Data & Account Deletion Policy.

8 Your Privacy Rights

As a resident using DWWH PG, you have the following rights:

  • Access & Review: You can review your profile, room allocations, leave records, and payment history at any time directly within the DWWH PG mobile application.
  • Rectification: You can request corrections to any inaccurate contact information or emergency contacts through the app or by notifying management.
  • Account Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and personal profile both within the app settings and via our online web request channel.

9 Age Eligibility & Children's Privacy

Our services and mobile application are designed for adult residents, working professionals, and students who have reached the age of majority (18 years or older) or college students with parental consent. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
