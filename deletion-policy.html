Account & Data Management

User Data & Account Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play Store & Apple App Store user data policies, learn how you can request the deletion of your DWWH PG account and associated personal data.

App Name: DWWH PG (Flutter) • Developer / Host: Divya Women's Hostel (DWWH PG) • Turnaround: Within 30 Days • Support: dwwh.chennai@gmail.com

1 Overview of Your Deletion Rights

At DWWH PG, we respect your privacy and provide transparent control over your personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your resident account, authentication credentials, and personal information at any time upon vacating the premises or discontinuing the use of our services.

Under Google Play and Apple App Store guidelines, we provide two simple methods to initiate account and data deletion: directly within the DWWH PG mobile application, or online via web/email without needing to have the mobile app currently installed.

2 How to Request Account & Data Deletion

💡

Note on Active Tenancy:

If you are currently staying at DWWH PG with an active room allocation or pending rental dues/security deposit refunds, please initiate a vacation notice through the app or coordinate with hostel administration so pending accounts and security deposits can be settled prior to account termination.

Method A: Request Deletion Directly Inside the DWWH PG App

1
Open App & Log In
Open the DWWH PG Flutter mobile application on your Android or iOS device and log in using your registered mobile number/email.
2
Go to Profile / Settings
Tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar and select Account Settings.
3
Select "Delete Account"
Scroll to the bottom of the Account Settings page and tap "Request Account Deletion".
4
Confirm Your Request
Review the confirmation notice detailing the data deletion process and confirm. Our automated system will queue your request immediately.

Method B: Request Deletion via Web / Email (No App Installation Required)

If you have already uninstalled the mobile application or no longer have access to your device, you can submit an account and data deletion request directly via email to our administration team.

Submit Deletion Request by Email

Send an email from your registered email address to dwwh.chennai@gmail.com with the details below:

To: dwwh.chennai@gmail.com
Subject: DWWH PG - Account and Data Deletion Request

Hello DWWH PG Management,

I am requesting the permanent deletion of my DWWH PG account and associated personal data.
• Registered Full Name: [Your Name]

• Room/Bed Number (if applicable): [Your Room/Bed]

3 Detailed Data Treatment Breakdown

In compliance with data protection laws and statutory accounting standards, below is a complete breakdown of what data is permanently deleted and what data must be retained for legitimate legal and regulatory compliance:

Data Category Specific Items Action Taken Retention Period / Legal Reason
User Authentication & Access Password hashes, login tokens, active session IDs, device tokens (Firebase FCM). Permanently Deleted Immediately upon verification (within 7 business days). Revokes all app access.
Profile & Media Resident profile photo, emergency contact links, uploaded room complaint photos. Permanently Deleted Purged from cloud storage and database within 30 days.
Leave & Gate Pass Logs Routine gate pass entries, vacation requests, and leave approval records. Permanently Deleted Purged within 30 days of confirmed move-out and account deletion.
Tenancy & KYC Verification Proofs Government ID copies (Aadhaar/Voter ID) and police tenant verification copies. Retained Temporarily Up to 180 Days: Retained strictly to comply with local police station tenant verification laws and security audit guidelines in Tamil Nadu / India, after which documents are permanently scrubbed.
Financial Ledgers & Tax Receipts Rental invoices, security deposit transactions, refund records, and tax receipts. Retained for Compliance Statutory Requirement: Retained for financial, tax, and GST audit compliance as mandated under the Indian Income Tax Act & Companies Act (up to 7 years). These records are strictly archived and never used for marketing.

4 Processing Timeline & Verification

  • Identity Verification (48 - 72 Hours): Upon receiving your web/email request, our administration will verify that the request originated from the authentic account holder to prevent unauthorized account terminations.
  • Clearance Check: We verify that all keys have been returned, room dues settled, and the refundable security deposit balance has been refunded or resolved.
  • Permanent Deletion (Within 30 Days): All eligible user records, login credentials, and media files will be irrevocably purged from active production databases in accordance with Google Play's 30-day timeline.
  • Completion Confirmation: You will receive an official email confirmation once your account deletion and data scrubbing are finalized.
